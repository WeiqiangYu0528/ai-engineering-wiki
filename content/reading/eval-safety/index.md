Each entry assumes the ones above it, which is the only claim this page makes — it is a route in, not a table of contents. The [Evaluation & Safety map](/eval-safety/) has the full list.

1. [Evaluation](/eval-safety/concepts/evaluation) — measurement first, because every claim further down this path is a claim about a number somebody had to define
2. [Hallucination](/eval-safety/concepts/hallucination) — the failure mode a reader meets first, and the one step 1 is worst at catching
3. [LLM Bias](/eval-safety/concepts/llm-bias) — systematic skew rather than one-off error, which is why it needs its own measurement design
4. [Adversarial Prompting](/eval-safety/concepts/adversarial-prompting) — failures induced on purpose, once you can recognize the ones that occur by themselves
5. [Prompt Injection](/eval-safety/concepts/prompt-injection) — the case of step 4 that stops being theoretical the moment tools are in the loop
6. [Interpretability](/eval-safety/concepts/interpretability) — why the failures in 2–5 happen, attempted after they can be named
7. [AI Ethics](/eval-safety/concepts/ai-ethics) — the widest lens, and the one that reads as hand-waving without 1–6 underneath it

Every page here names the one before it in its header and links the one after it at the foot of its body, so the order can be followed without coming back to this page.
