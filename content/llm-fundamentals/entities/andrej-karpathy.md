## Overview
**Andrej Karpathy** is an influential AI researcher, educator, and founding member of [[openai]]. Formerly the Senior Director of AI at Tesla (leading Autopilot vision and neural network development), Karpathy is globally renowned for his deep pedagogical contributions, open-source educational codebases (such as `nanoGPT`, `llm.c`, and `micrograd`), and formulation of key AI mental models (e.g., Software 2.0, LLM OS, and the LLM Wiki pattern).

## Key Facts
- **OpenAI Founding Member:** Worked on generative models, reinforcement learning, and early transformer scaling.
- **Tesla Autopilot AI Lead:** Spearheaded full self-driving computer vision systems and distributed training infrastructure.
- **Eureka Labs Founder:** Founded Eureka Labs in 2024 to pioneer AI-native education platforms.
- **Landmark Educational Work:** Creator of the CS231n course at Stanford, `nanoGPT`, `llm.c` (training GPT in pure C/CUDA), and long-form foundational lectures on LLMs.

## Significance in AI Engineering
- Formulated the **"LLM OS"** conceptual architecture (LLM as CPU, context window as RAM, tools as peripherals).
- Designed the **"LLM Wiki"** architectural pattern for building compounding, AI-maintained personal knowledge bases.
- Set the global standard for first-principles, code-first AI education.
