## Overview
**Meta (formerly Facebook)** is the parent company of **AI @ Meta (FAIR / GenAI)** — the organization behind the open-weight **[[llama-3]]** herd (8B/70B/405B), Llama 2, and Llama Guard safety models, detailed in [[source-llama-3-herd-of-models]]. Its 2024 report establishes Meta as the leading open frontier lab alongside [[openai]] and [[anthropic]], releasing 405B-class models under the Llama 3 Community License.

## Key Facts
- **Labs:** FAIR (Fundamental AI Research) + GenAI org; creators of PyTorch, RoBERTa, OPT, Llama family.
- **Open strategy:** Llama 3 (15.6T tokens, 405B params, 128K context, 3.8e25 FLOPs) and Llama Guard 3 — weights + report openly released.
- **Infrastructure:** Grand Teton H100 clusters, Tectonic storage, RoCE fabric — production scale for 16K-GPU training.

## Significance in AI Engineering
- Defines the open frontier: Llama 3 405B matches GPT-4/4o class while remaining fine-tunable and self-hostable.
- Sets the public benchmark for pre-training data curation (15T pipeline) and inference-optimal overtraining.
