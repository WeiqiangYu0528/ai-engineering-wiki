## Overview
**Jay Alammar** is an AI researcher, visual educator, and author renowned for his visual explanations of deep learning concepts and Large Language Models. His blog posts (*The Illustrated Transformer*, *The Illustrated Word2vec*, *The Illustrated Stable Diffusion*, *The Illustrated GPT-2*) are among the most cited educational resources in AI, utilized in university courses at Stanford, MIT, Harvard, CMU, and Princeton.

## Key Facts
- **Author of "The Illustrated Transformer" (2018):** Published [[source-the-illustrated-transformer]], which created the definitive visual mental model for self-attention, encoder-decoder representations, and matrix computations in Transformers.
- **Book Author:** Co-author of *Hands-On Large Language Models* (O'Reilly Media, 2024 with Maarten Grootendorst), covering modern LLM architectures, fine-tuning, RAG, and multimodal pipelines.
- **Visual Educator & Industry Leader:** Co-creator of Cohere LLM University and former Director at Cohere, driving open educational curricula for enterprise LLM adoption.

## Significance in AI Engineering
- Pioneered the visual pedagogy of modern natural language processing, establishing standard diagrams for vector embeddings, query-key-value projections, and multi-head attention spaces.
